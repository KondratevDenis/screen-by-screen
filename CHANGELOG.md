# Changelog

## 1.0.2 — 2026-09-23

The group's `screen` is a route, not a snapshot of one walk: no entity ids in the path, enter a
state by the regular path rather than by its address, and confirm arrival with a sign that answers
"am I here" instead of a count. The sweep before handoff walks `screen` rather than finding its own
way back.
